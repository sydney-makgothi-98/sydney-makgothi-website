:::worked The budget of a frame
A display refreshes at 144 hertz. The robot page of this website redraws a three-dimensional scene and eight charts. What is the time budget for one frame, and how much remains if the browser itself needs about 3 milliseconds for style, layout and compositing?

**Step 1. Budget.** The period is the reciprocal of the frequency: 1000 ms ÷ 144 = 6.94 ms.

**Step 2. Remaining time for your own code.** 6.94 − 3 = 3.94 ms.

**Step 3. Compare with 60 hertz.** At 60 Hz the budget is 16.67 ms, which leaves 13.67 ms for the same code, more than three times as much. A page that is comfortable at 60 Hz can be over budget at 144 Hz.

**What happens when you exceed the budget?** The browser does not squeeze the work in. It misses the frame and shows the previous one again, and the motion appears to stutter. This is why the robot page measures its own frame times and, if they stay too long, lowers the rendering quality one step at a time until it fits.
:::
